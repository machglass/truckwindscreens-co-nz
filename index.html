Welcome to Truck Windscreens

Our family has been supplying auto glass in New Zealand since 1967. The second generation in 1989 added full service with mobile and in shop installations.

We have been direct billing insurance companies for a long time. After all these years we are still NZ and family owned.

We replace truck windscreens, door glass, side glass and rear glass, with a mobile service where we come to you or in-store at 133c Pilkington Road, Panmure, Auckland 1072.

Call us today on 0800 321 800 and speak with a friendly professional.
